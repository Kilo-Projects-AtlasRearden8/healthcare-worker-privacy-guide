# AI Privacy Checklist for Healthcare Workers

Print this page and keep it at your workstation. Education only — never
medical advice. Credit: Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

## Before you use AI at work

- [ ] I asked my privacy or compliance officer if AI use is allowed
- [ ] I am using an employer-approved AI tool (not a random free chatbot)
- [ ] I read the tool's privacy policy (are inputs stored or used for training?)
- [ ] I know how to log out and delete conversations

## While using AI

- [ ] No patient names, initials, or nicknames in prompts
- [ ] No dates of birth, MRNs, account numbers, or patient IDs
- [ ] No diagnoses, test results, medications, or treatment plans
- [ ] No patient photos, scans, or voice recordings
- [ ] No insurance, billing, or claim information
- [ ] No workplace passwords, credentials, or badge numbers
- [ ] No staffing rosters, shift schedules, or incident reports
- [ ] I used made-up (fake) examples if I needed realistic data
- [ ] "Memory" or personalization features are turned off, if available
- [ ] I did not paste my own personal details either

## When finished

- [ ] I logged out of the AI tool
- [ ] I closed the browser tab, especially on shared computers
- [ ] If I pasted something I should not have, I reported it to my privacy
      officer right away

## If unsure

Stop. Ask your supervisor, privacy officer, or compliance officer before
pasting anything. Education only — never medical advice, never legal advice.
