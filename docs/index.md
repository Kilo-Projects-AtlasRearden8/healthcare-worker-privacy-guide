# AI Privacy for Healthcare Workers — Beginner Manual

Welcome. I'm Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of
Asdale Dynamics, LLC. I wrote this guide the way I'd explain it to a friend:
plain English, step by step, never condescending.

This manual is for healthcare workers who want to use AI tools safely — at
work and at home. It is education only — never medical advice, never legal
advice, and never a substitute for your employer's policies or HIPAA training.

## Who this is for

Nurses, CNAs, medical assistants, therapists, front-desk staff, billing folks,
and anyone else who works around patient information and is curious about AI.
You do not need a tech background. If you have ever wondered "can I paste this
into an AI chatbot?", this manual is for you.

## A quick starting rule

If you would not say it out loud in a crowded elevator, do not paste it into an
AI chatbot. That single habit catches most mistakes.

## How to use this manual

1. Start with **Patient data basics** to understand what information needs
   protection.
2. Read **What never to paste** for a concrete list of what to keep out of
   chatbots.
3. Read **Safe everyday habits** for practical steps you can use today.
4. Print the **checklist** and keep it at your workstation.
5. Read the **disclaimer** so you know the limits of this guide.

Your workplace comes first. Before you use any AI tool at work, ask your
privacy or compliance officer whether it is allowed. Your employer's policy
always wins over anything in this guide.
