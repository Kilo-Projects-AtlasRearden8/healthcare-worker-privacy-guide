# AI Privacy for Healthcare Workers — Beginner Manual

Welcome. I'm Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of
Asdale Dynamics, LLC. This guide is written like I'd explain it to a friend:
plain English, step by step, never condescending.

It is for healthcare workers who want to use AI tools safely at work and at
home. It is education only — never medical advice, never legal advice, and
never a substitute for your employer's policies or HIPAA training.

## Who this is for

Nurses, CNAs, medical assistants, therapists, front-desk staff, billing folks,
and anyone else who works around patient information and is curious about AI.
You do not need a tech background.

## A quick starting rule

If you would not say it out loud in a crowded elevator, do not paste it into an
AI chatbot. That single habit catches most mistakes.
