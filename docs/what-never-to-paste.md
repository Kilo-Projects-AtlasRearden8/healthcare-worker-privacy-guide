# What Never to Paste Into AI Chatbots

If you would not say it out loud in a crowded elevator, do not paste it into an
AI chatbot. Here is a concrete never-paste list for healthcare workers.

## Never paste

- Patient names — first, last, or initials
- Dates of birth, ages combined with other details, admission or discharge dates
- Medical record numbers (MRNs), account numbers, patient IDs
- Diagnoses, conditions, test results, lab values
- Medications, dosages, treatment plans, surgery details
- Photos, scans, X-rays, EKGs, voice recordings, wound photos
- Insurance info, billing codes, claim numbers
- Anything that identifies a patient's family members, employer, or address
- Your workplace login credentials, passwords, or badge numbers
- Internal notes, shift schedules, staffing rosters, incident reports
- Anything covered by confidentiality agreements or your employer's policy

## The "small detail" trap

You might think "I'll just paste the lab value, not the name." That is still
risky. A rare diagnosis plus a shift, unit, or age can identify a patient just
as well as a name. De-identifying by hand is hard — do not assume you did it
right.

## Examples: safe vs. not safe

Not safe:
> "Here is a progress note for Mr. Alvarez, DOB 03/14/1972, MRN 88421. Can you
> rewrite it?"

Not safe:
> "A 68-year-old on the night shift at County General with a rare heart
> condition — what does this lab result mean?"

Safer:
> "Can you explain what a normal potassium range is, in plain English?"

Safer:
> "Help me write a polite appointment reminder script for patients."

If you need to test an AI with real-looking data, make up fake patient details
yourself — never reuse real ones.

## What to do if you already pasted something

1. Stop pasting more
2. Do not panic — act quickly
3. Tell your supervisor, privacy officer, or compliance officer right away
4. Follow your employer's incident reporting process
5. Ask whether the chat conversation can be deleted in the AI tool's settings

Reporting fast is almost always better than hiding it.
