# AI Privacy for Healthcare Workers

A beginner manual and static HTML site on AI privacy for healthcare workers:
what never to paste into chatbots, patient data basics, and safe habits.
Education only — never medical advice. Includes a printable checklist.

Written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of
**Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). Dylan explains AI
"like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. This
guide follows that same voice.

Asdale Dynamics, LLC focuses on beginner-friendly AI education, books, and
guides. Hub site: dylanasdale.github.io

## Who this is for

Nurses, CNAs, medical assistants, therapists, front-desk staff, billing folks,
and anyone else who works around patient information and is curious about using
AI tools safely. You do not need a tech background. If you've ever wondered
"can I paste this into an AI chatbot?", this manual is for you.

## How to use

1. Read the Markdown files in `docs/` in any text editor, or
2. Open any `.html` file in `site/` directly in your web browser.

No server, no install, no account, and no internet required once you have the
files. To print the checklist, open `site/checklist.html` and use your
browser's print dialog — the print stylesheet strips out navigation and
formats the checklist to fit one page.

## Important: education only, never medical advice

This manual teaches privacy habits for using AI around patient information. It
is **not medical advice**, not legal advice, not compliance advice, and not a
substitute for your employer's policies or HIPAA training. Always follow your
workplace's rules and ask your privacy or compliance officer when unsure.
